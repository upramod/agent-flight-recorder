# AI-assistance disclosure

Prepared for the USENIX Security 2027 submission record.

Generative AI tools were used during this project for drafting and editing manuscript text, reviewing technical wording, assisting with repository and CI scripting, and checking the presentation of analyses. The research claims, experimental design decisions, frozen policy boundaries, benchmark execution, and reported evidence remain the responsibility of the human author.

The confirmatory experimental observations were produced by the recorded AgentDojo benchmark workflows rather than invented by a generative AI system. Reported counts, paired tables, confidence intervals, and McNemar p-values were checked against frozen artifacts and independently recomputed before the submission candidate was frozen. References were checked against primary sources.

Before external submission, the human author must review the complete manuscript and artifact package and accept responsibility for their accuracy, originality, and integrity.

USENIX Security 2027 permits AI tools to assist in preparing submissions and states that human authors remain responsible for submitted content and results. The final submission must follow the policy in force on the submission date.
